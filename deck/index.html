03 The solution

An AI-native company that keeps your community safe 24/7

Haven plugs into your community and runs a full Trust & Safety team of AI employees — detect, investigate, care, and learn — with a human founder approving every ban.

Detect in <1s Nova

Nova screens every message through a RocketRide triage pipeline — sub-millisecond fast path — and quarantines threats instantly.

Investigate with evidence Rex

Rex detonates links in an isolated Celesto computer via an AdaL browser workflow: screenshots, redirects, forms, lookalike domains.

Care for humans Mira

Mira — real face & voice via Masky.AI — answers crisis messages in seconds with 988 / findahelpline resources and pages a human via n8n. Crisis messages are never auto-deleted.

05 How it works

One closed loop. Every incident makes Haven smarter.

  1. Community message — Discord, Slack, Telegram
  2. Nova — RocketRide triage, <1ms fast path
  3. BAND — agent-to-agent bus
  4. Aria routes — fans out to specialists
  5. Rex · Mira · Juno · Kai — AdaL + Celesto, Masky + n8n, Prelint, Snyk + Tenki
  6. Founder approval — one click
  7. n8n enforcement log
  8. PromptQL learning loop
  9. Finch skills · Paritok compression — back to the community

Models via Flatkey fastreasoningcodingvision with failover and per-call cost tracking

07 Safety & trust

Safety & trust, by design

Human-in-the-loop

Every ban needs founder approval — one click, with the evidence attached.

Crisis protocol

Crisis messages are never auto-deleted, always escalate to a human, and include 988 / findahelpline.

PII redaction

Names, emails, phones and IDs are redacted before any model call.

Policy as specs

Community rules live as specs and every change is checked by Prelint.

Snyk-gated CI on Tenki runners

No build ships with known-vulnerable dependencies: Snyk scans gate every merge, run on isolated Tenki CI runners.
