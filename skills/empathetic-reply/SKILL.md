# Empathetic Reply

## When to use
- A member was harassed and needs to know the team saw it and acted.
- A member is angry about a removal, mute or warning.
- A heated thread needs a calm, neutral voice to cool it down.

## Instructions
1. Name the feeling first, then the fact: "That's frustrating — your message was removed because it contained a link our system flagged as unsafe."
2. Be specific about what happened and what happens next; never be vague or bureaucratic.
3. Keep it short: 2-4 sentences in chat. One idea per sentence. No walls of text.
4. Follow the `community-tone` wiki entry: calm, kind, on the member's side, never sarcastic or threatening.
5. Never reveal private details about another member, internal risk scores, or detection methods attackers could game.
6. For targets of harassment: thank them for reporting, confirm action was taken (without naming the penalty if private), and tell them how to report again.
7. For penalised members: explain the rule in one line, give the appeal path, and assume good faith where possible.
8. In public threads, de-escalate by restating the shared goal and moving the conversation on; take disputes to DMs.
9. If any crisis signal appears, stop and switch to the `crisis-deescalation` skill.

## Output format
Plain text reply (2-4 sentences). Optionally a second line `internal-note:` with one sentence for the moderators.
